AI alignment is the challenge of ensuring that AI systems pursue the goals and values their designers intend, particularly as systems become more capable.

The core concern is that a sufficiently capable AI optimizing for a proxy goal might find unexpected ways to achieve it that diverge from human values — a phenomenon sometimes called "goal misgeneralization" or "reward hacking."

Key subproblems include:
- **Value specification**: Correctly defining what we want AI systems to optimize for
- **Value learning**: Teaching AI to infer human values from behavior
- **Corrigibility**: Ensuring AI systems remain controllable and correctable
- **Robustness**: Maintaining aligned behavior across distribution shifts
