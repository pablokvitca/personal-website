The AI safety mindset is a way of thinking about AI systems that focuses on failure modes and unexpected behaviors rather than assuming the system will work as intended.

Borrowed from the security mindset (thinking like an attacker), it asks: "How might this system fail catastrophically? What edge cases could cause it to optimize in unintended ways?"

Practitioners of the safety mindset:
- Assume the AI will find loopholes in specifications
- Think adversarially about reward functions and objectives
- Design for robustness against distribution shift
- Prefer conservative, verifiable behavior over peak performance
