Machine learning is the study of algorithms that learn from data. Rather than encoding rules explicitly, ML systems identify patterns in training examples and generalize to new inputs.

Major paradigms:
- **Supervised learning**: Learning from labeled input-output pairs
- **Unsupervised learning**: Finding structure in unlabeled data
- **Reinforcement learning**: Learning via rewards and penalties from an environment
- **Self-supervised learning**: Using the data itself to generate training signal (e.g., predicting masked tokens)

Modern large language models are trained with a combination of self-supervised pre-training and reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF).
